\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
A NURBS surface is a type of parametric geometry. A NURBS surface is defined by the order, form, knot vector and control points in the U and V coordinates.

For more information on the meaning of form, knot vectors and control points, see the \doxylink{class_fbx_nurbs_curve}{Fbx\+Nurbs\+Curve} documentation. The same rules apply for the NURBS curves and NURBS surfaces, but NURBS surfaces have two dimensions (U and V).
